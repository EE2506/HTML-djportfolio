Making a small business website earn its keep

A sample note about designing a website to help with an actual business conversation, not simply to occupy a URL.

Sample contentThis unlisted page uses placeholder writing only.

A small-business website does not need to do everything. It does need to make the next useful step obvious. That could be a message, an appointment, a product question, or a visit to the store.

Start with the real question

Before choosing sections or effects, ask what people are trying to learn when they arrive. A helpful answer is often more valuable than another paragraph about the company.

For a local service business, that could mean showing the service area, a simple way to inquire, and what happens after someone reaches out.

Give the next action a place to land

A clear call to action should feel like the natural continuation of the page. It does not need to be loud, but it should not be difficult to find.
